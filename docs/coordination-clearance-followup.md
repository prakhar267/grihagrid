# Coordination clearance and renderer recovery

Customer problem: a designer-entered column can pass through a bed without a
coordination warning, and a blocked local-renderer health request can leave the
export panel checking indefinitely.

Journey: open the affected floor → inspect an actionable furniture intersection
→ select and reposition the entered component → review and accept the study →
rebuild the tour and export the shared geometry. A renderer that cannot respond
must expose setup and a retry without losing the house or existing render jobs.

Acceptance: same-floor component/furniture envelopes honor rotation and base
height; separate floors, touching surfaces and overhead components do not create
false warnings. No geometry or engineered sizes change automatically. The
renderer health check settles within eight seconds, is cancelled on unmount or
pairing, and can be retried. Measure success by the reported fixture collision
being detected and resolved in the browser, with no indefinite checking state.

These are envelope-intersection checks, not ergonomic clearance, structural
capacity, circuit design, hydraulic sizing or municipal certification. Keep the
existing release controls, Cloudflare-first AI, commerce/upload closures and
device-test deferrals.
