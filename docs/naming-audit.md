# GrihaGrid naming audit

## Founder verdict

**Conditional go — 7/10.** `GrihaGrid` is a relevant, memorable working name
for an India-first home-planning product. It is not a legal clearance. Hold
meaningful paid acquisition, long-term signage, and major PR until an Indian
trademark professional completes exact, phonetic, and related-mark searches.

## Why the name works

- *Gṛha* means house, habitation, or home; “Grid” signals plans, structure, and
  systematic decision-making.
- The compound supports the intended position: home-planning intelligence
  before architectural and construction commitments.
- Use the spelling **GrihaGrid** consistently. Spoken and search variants such
  as “Grih” and “Gruha” are likely, so those variants belong in clearance and
  defensive-domain searches.

## Material risk

The adjacent name is **GRIHA**, the nationally recognized Green Rating for
Integrated Habitat Assessment operated by GRIHA Council. It occupies the same
built-environment context, so typography or claims must not imply certification,
rating, or affiliation. See [GRIHA Rating](https://www.grihaindia.org/griha-rating)
and the [GRIHA Council](https://grihaindia.org/).

Brand guardrails:

- Write **GrihaGrid**, not “GRIHA Grid.”
- Pair it with a plain descriptor such as “Home planning intelligence.”
- Avoid green-rating stars, certification badges, and claims that resemble a
  building rating.
- Keep the current architectural-monograph visual system distinct from rating
  or certification products.

## Point-in-time availability screen

As of 13 August 2026 IST, an initial web screen found no obvious exact-name
consumer product. NIXI RDAP returned `grihagrid.in` as available at the time of
the check; availability can change immediately. Recheck through
[NIXI RDAP](https://rdap.nixiregistry.in/rdap/domain/grihagrid.in) immediately
before purchase. Exact GitHub and X profile URLs also returned 404, while other
social networks were inconclusive. None of these results reserves a name,
domain, or handle.

## Clearance sequence before paid growth

1. Search `GRIHAGRID`, `GRIHA GRID`, `GRUHA GRID`, `GRIH GRID`, and phonetic
   variants through the official [IP India trademark search guidance](https://www.ipindia.gov.in/trade-marks-before-you-apply-search-existing-trademarks).
2. Prioritize Nice Class 42 for SaaS, software design, architectural, and
   engineering services, plus Class 9 for downloadable software or plan files.
   Review Classes 35 and 37 if the model expands into a marketplace or actual
   construction. See the WIPO class descriptions for
   [42](https://nclpub.wipo.int/enfr/?basic_numbers=show&class_number=42),
   [9](https://nclpub.wipo.int/enfr/?basic_numbers=show&class_number=9),
   [35](https://nclpub.wipo.int/enfr/?basic_numbers=show&class_number=35), and
   [37](https://nclpub.wipo.int/enfr/?basic_numbers=show&class_number=37).
3. Run MCA company and LLP name searches; spacing, letter case, and punctuation
   may not distinguish names. Review [MCA RUN guidance](https://www.mca.gov.in/Ministry/pdf/RUN_help.pdf).
4. Ask Indian trademark counsel specifically about proximity to GRIHA Council.
5. If counsel gives a go, reserve the `.in`, `.com`, and consistent social
   handles in one coordinated step.

## Decision

Continue controlled product testing under GrihaGrid. Do not describe the mark as
cleared or registered until the official searches and counsel review are done.
