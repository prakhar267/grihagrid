# Reference experience audit

## Scope

Combined UX/accessibility review of the public acquisition, pricing and account-entry flow at `homesforge.com`, captured on 12 August 2026 in desktop and 390 × 844 mobile states. Evidence is stored in `/Users/prakhar/Desktop/HouseForge/reference-audit/`.

## Flow steps

1. **Landing hero — healthy.** The outcome and paid/free distinction are immediately visible. The dark, low-contrast supporting text and promotion overlay are accessibility/readability risks.
2. **How it works — healthy.** Four steps make an unfamiliar AI/professional hybrid easy to understand. “Construction-ready” language should be narrowed to avoid overstating an indicative product.
3. **Plot-fit calculator — strong.** This is the best activation surface: it turns abstract value into a personal answer. Slider-only precision and compact touch targets need keyboard/mobile testing.
4. **Cost calculator — strong.** City and quality controls explain why estimates vary. Assumptions should be timestamped and ranges should never look like quotes.
5. **Report contents — healthy.** The output is concrete and supports planning conversations. Compliance and Vastu labels need visible limitation text.
6. **Pricing — needs simplification.** One-time pricing matches the episodic job, but four dense cards create comparison work and badges compete for attention.
7. **Registration — healthy.** The form is short and mobile-friendly. Password creation before a prospect sees their saved result adds friction; magic-link or brief-first onboarding would be lower effort.
8. **FAQ/legal reassurance — healthy with verification gaps.** Scope, refund and data questions are addressed, but screenshots cannot prove implementation, retention behavior or WCAG conformance.

## Highest-impact recommendations adopted in GrihaGrid

- Sell decision confidence, not autonomous architectural authority.
- Let users complete a brief before the account/paywall moment.
- Keep a free live feasibility tool as the acquisition wedge.
- Use one clearly recommended paid plan and reserve expert review for high-stakes confidence.
- Make privacy, payment, data deletion and professional-scope boundaries part of the product, not footer-only copy.
- Track report generation and review as explicit lifecycle states with retry-safe operations.

## Evidence limits

The public reference could be captured without authentication. Paid checkout, dashboard, report quality, upload privacy, error recovery, keyboard focus, assistive-technology behavior and backend reliability could not be verified from public screenshots alone.
